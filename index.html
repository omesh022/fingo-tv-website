About Fingo TV

Fingo TV does not provide, host, distribute or sell any media content, channels, streams or subscriptions. The app is distributed empty. You add your own playlist or Xtream Codes login for sources you are legally authorized to access, and the app plays them for you.

There are no accounts, no developer backend or cloud server, and no tracking. Your playlists, favorites and settings stay on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.
