Last updated: October 17, 2026

Introduction

Fingo TV is an Android media player and playlist (IPTV) player. It does not provide, host, distribute or sell any media content, channels, streams or subscriptions. The app is distributed empty. You add your own playlist or Xtream Codes login for sources you are legally authorized to access, and the app loads and plays them for you.

This policy covers the Fingo TV Android app and this website. This website is a set of static pages: it sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

Developer and Contact

Fingo TV is developed by Omesh Kumar. For support, privacy and copyright inquiries, contact info@wdmart.online.

Information We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect information from the app. Specifically:

  • There is no developer backend or cloud server for the app to send data to.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising SDK, no tracking and no telemetry in the app.
  • Your information is not sold or shared with the developer or with advertisers, because the developer never receives it.

Information Stored On Your Device

So the app can work, the following are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Playlist URLs and Xtream Codes server details you add.
  • Channel and program metadata loaded from your playlists.
  • Favorites and your watch later list.
  • Playback history.
  • App preferences and settings.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer.

Third-Party Servers You Choose

The app connects only to the servers you configure: your playlist host or your Xtream Codes server. It connects to those servers to load your lists and to play your streams.

Like any connection over the internet, those servers may receive your IP address, your device's user-agent, and the login details you entered for that server. The developer has no control over those third-party servers or providers, and their handling of your information is governed by their own policies and terms. Only add servers and providers you trust and are authorized to use.

OpenSubtitles

When you choose "Search online" for subtitles, the app sends the content title, year, and season and episode where relevant, to the OpenSubtitles API at opensubtitles.com so it can search for matching subtitles. Nothing is sent unless you choose that option. You can use "Load from file" instead to load a subtitle file from your device, which sends nothing online.

Credential Security

When you save an Xtream Codes login, your password is encrypted with AES-256 GCM using the hardware-backed Android KeyStore on your device. Credentials are never logged and never sent to the developer. They are sent only to the server you configured, so the app can sign in on your behalf. When you remove a playlist, its saved credentials are deleted.

Data Deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are in control of deleting it:

  • Delete a playlist in the app to remove it and its saved credentials.
  • Clear watch history and search history in Settings.
  • Clear the app's data in Android system settings to remove everything at once.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on a server.

Children's Privacy

Fingo TV is not intended for users under 18. The app does not knowingly collect information from children. As explained above, the app does not collect information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be announced in the app and on this site.
